# First 7 Days Progression

| Day | Expected state | Main choices |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Claim tutorial rewards, assign workers, upgrade Warehouse or City Hall, possibly start Academy. | Storage safety vs faster city growth. |
| 2 | Build Tavern, improve happiness, assign scientists. | More workers vs research generation. |
| 3 | Complete first research, build Barracks, train first Spearmen. | Economy spend vs military spend. |
| 4 | Attack level 1 Barbarian camp, read battle report, continue research. | PvE timing vs city upgrades. |
| 5 | Train mixed army, attack stronger camp, prepare Palace resources. | Army growth vs expansion savings. |
| 6 | Complete Palace level 1, prepare colonization resources, choose target slot. | Second city location. |
| 7 | Start or complete colonization, manage second city, see next empire goal. | Capital growth vs colony support. |

First iteration verdict:

- No hard blocker should appear before Academy, Barracks, or Palace.
- The player should always have one useful short action and one longer-term target.
- Debug dashboard and test accounts cover Day 1, Day 2-3, Day 4, and Day 6-7 states.
