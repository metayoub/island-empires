# PvE Rewards

| Camp level | Enemy strength | Victory losses | Rewards |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 40 | 10% | 500 wood, 250 gold |
| 2 | 120 | 20% | 900 wood, 500 gold |
| 3 | 260 | 30% | 1,500 wood, 900 gold |
| 10 | 1,065 | 32.1% | 9,000 wood, 4,500 gold, 2,500 marble, 500 sulfur |

First iteration notes:

- Level 1 camp should be approachable with 6-8 Spearmen.
- Level 2 should require mixed army planning.
- Level 3 should be visible as an aspirational first-week challenge, not the default farm.
- Level 10 starts supporting advanced city upgrades with marble and sulfur in the loot table.
