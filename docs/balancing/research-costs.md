# Research Costs

Current research production is 5 points per scientist per hour, with +25% per Academy level above 1.

The cost curve is intentionally much higher than the first iteration. A player bank of roughly
14,800 research points after a couple of days should feel strong, but it should not clear the
medium-game tree by itself. Academy upgrades and scientist assignment are now expected to be
core progression choices, not optional optimizations.

| Tier | Cost | Time | Product intent |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 900 | 30m | First meaningful choices; reachable with early Academy/scientists. |
| 2 | 3,200 | 2h | Branch specialization; players should choose economy, military, naval, or governance. |
| 3 | 8,200 | 6h | Midgame commitments; Academy upgrades strongly affect pacing. |
| 4 | 16,500 | 12h | Strategic unlocks; requires sustained scientist investment. |

Production examples:

| Academy level | 5 scientists | 10 scientists | 20 scientists |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 25/hour | 50/hour | 100/hour |
| 2 | 31/hour | 62/hour | 125/hour |
| 5 | 50/hour | 100/hour | 200/hour |
| 10 | 81/hour | 162/hour | 325/hour |

Current notes:

- Research point generation and research timer duration both matter now.
- Academy level 1 is useful, but higher Academy levels create visible compounding value.
- Scientists compete with workers, so research acceleration has a real economy tradeoff.
