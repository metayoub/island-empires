# Research Costs

Current research production is 4 points per scientist per hour, with +10% per Academy level above 1.

| Technology | Tier | Cost | Time | Target day |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Improved Woodcutting | 1 | 50 | 60s | Day 2-3 |
| Basic Storage | 1 | 75 | 90s | Day 2-3 |
| Basic Training | 1 | 100 | 120s | Day 3 |
| Basic Navigation | 1 | 100 | 120s | Day 5-6 |
| Basic Architecture | 1 | 100 | 120s | Day 4-5 |
| Organized Labor | 2 | 180 | 210s | After first week |

First iteration notes:

- Research point generation, not research timer duration, is the main gate.
- Academy level 1 with 5 scientists produces 20 research points/hour.
- Basic Navigation is required before colonization, so it should be visible by Day 5.
