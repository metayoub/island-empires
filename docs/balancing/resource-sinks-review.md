# Resource Sinks Review

Current early sinks:

| Sink | Resources | First-week role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Building upgrades | Wood, gold | Primary planning sink |
| Unit training | Wood, gold, later sulfur | Competes with city growth |
| Research | Research points | Long-term progression |
| Colonization | Wood, gold | Major Day 6-7 milestone |

Findings:

- Wood is the strongest early pressure because buildings and units both consume it.
- Gold pressure is moderate and mainly rises when training units.
- Luxury resources are currently late or island-specific and should not gate the first 7 days.
- PvE rewards are useful but not high enough to replace production.

First iteration action:

- Keep Palace at 300 wood and 200 gold for level 1.
- Keep Spearman at 40 wood and 20 gold.
- Watch for storage cap waste through the debug blocker report.
