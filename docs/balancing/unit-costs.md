# Unit Costs

Early military should compete with city growth for wood and gold without making the economy unrecoverable.

| Unit | Cost | Training time | Power role | Target |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Militia | 25 wood, 15 gold | 20s | Cheap bodies | Emergency filler |
| Spearman | 40 wood, 20 gold | 30s | Early defense | First PvE army |
| Archer | 60 wood, 30 gold | 45s | Early attack | Mixed Day 5 army |
| Swordsman | 80 wood, 50 gold, 5 sulfur | 60s | Strong infantry | After first week |

First iteration notes:

- 6 Spearmen cost 240 wood and 120 gold, a meaningful but recoverable Day 3-4 spend.
- Barbarian camp rewards grant resources into inventory, so PvE feels rewarding without overflowing city storage. Level 10+ camps start adding luxury resources for advanced city upgrades.
- Archer and Swordsman unlocks keep military progression from being instant.
