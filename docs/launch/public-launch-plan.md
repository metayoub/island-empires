# Public Launch Plan

## Beta Goals

- Validate registration and login reliability.
- Confirm players understand the tutorial.
- Measure D1 retention.
- Monitor early economy pacing.
- Validate combat, marketplace, and alliance activity.
- Collect private player feedback.
- Confirm admin, moderation, and live-ops workflows.

## Success Criteria

- Registration success rate: 95% or higher.
- Tutorial completion rate: 60% or higher.
- D1 retention: 25% or higher.
- Critical P0 bugs: 0 open.
- P1 progression blockers: 0 open.
- Average API error rate: under 1%.
- No severe economy exploit active.
- No unresolved payment issue if monetization is enabled.

## Launch Decision Checklist

- Beta metrics meet success criteria.
- Critical bug queue is clear.
- Balance changes are documented.
- Known issues are reviewed and acceptable.
- Support and moderation coverage are scheduled.
- Rollback plan is current.
- Launch communication is approved.

## Public Launch World Plan

Public launch may use a fresh world to avoid unfair early beta advantage and to allow final balance changes. If beta progress is reset, communicate the decision before public launch.

## Marketing Tasks

- Prepare launch announcement.
- Prepare store/community page copy where applicable.
- Prepare screenshots and short gameplay summary.
- Invite beta players to share feedback after public launch.

## Support Coverage

- Monitor registration, password reset, and verification issues during launch day.
- Review support tickets and beta feedback at least daily for the first launch week.
- Escalate account, payment, security, and data-loss issues immediately.

## Moderation Coverage

- Monitor message reports, abuse flags, suspicious trades, and repeated attack reports.
- Keep admin action logs reviewed for sensitive actions.
- Pause cohort growth if moderation load exceeds coverage.

## Known Issues

Use [Beta Known Issues](beta-known-issues.md) as the source of truth before launch.

## Rollback Plan

- Keep the production rollback runbook current.
- Pause registration if new players are blocked.
- Disable invite codes or close beta access if a critical issue appears.
- Revert unsafe balance changes through the documented deploy process.

## Launch Communication

Island Empires is opening its first public world after a limited beta. The beta helped validate onboarding, balance, trade, alliances, combat, and live operations. Some beta progress may be reset so public launch starts fairly.
